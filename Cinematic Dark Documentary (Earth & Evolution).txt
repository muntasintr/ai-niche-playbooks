* কন্টেন্ট স্ট্র্যাটেজি ও ফ্রিকোয়েন্সি: প্রতিটি video একটি prehistoric creature, ancient ecosystem, evolutionary mystery, catastrophic geological event, deep-ocean mystery, অথবা extreme survival scenario কেন্দ্র করে তৈরি হবে[cite: 3]।

৩. স্ক্রিপ্ট ব্লুপ্রিন্ট (Scriptwriting Framework)

* 0:00 - 0:45 (Atmospheric Hook): Begin with a terrifying or visually immense prehistoric moment. Establish scale, danger, and mystery immediately[cite: 3]।
* 0:45 - 3:00 (The Ancient World): Establish the geological period, environment, climate, and dominant lifeforms[cite: 3]।
* 3:00 - 7:00 (The Predator / Mystery): Introduce the main creature, evolutionary adaptation, or environmental threat[cite: 3]।
* 7:00 - 11:00 (The Struggle): Show predator-prey dynamics, environmental pressure, competition, or survival challenges[cite: 3]।
* 11:00 - 14:00 (Catastrophe / Evolution): Reveal the major environmental or evolutionary turning point[cite: 3]।
* 14:00 - 16:00 (Final Reflection): End with extinction, adaptation, evolution, or the transformation of Earth itself[cite: 3]।

  "You are a Master Cinematic Earth & Evolution Documentary Scriptwriter. Write a 16-minute cinematic documentary script for the title '[Insert Title]'. Tone: Grave, mysterious, philosophical, suspenseful, epic, and scientifically grounded. Voiceover: Extremely deep, heavy, authoritative documentary narration with slow pacing and dramatic pauses. Structure it following this framework: [0-45s: Atmospheric hook] [45s-3m: Ancient world] [3-7m: Predator / mystery] [7-11m: Struggle] [11-14m: Catastrophe / evolution] [14-16m: Final reflection]. Use scientifically grounded narration while maintaining a dark cinematic storytelling style."[cite: 3]
